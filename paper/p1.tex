\title[Realizations of tolerant B-reps]{When does a tolerant B-rep define a solid?\\
Existence, non-existence and uniqueness of realizations}
\author{Guoqing (Eddie) Zhang}
\date{Draft v0.7, 12 November 2026. Not for circulation.}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
Boundary representations in CAD kernels carry tolerances. Edge curves, the boundary curves of the adjacent faces,
and vertex points do not fit together exactly, and the solid they describe is only implied. We make this precise.
A \emph{realization} of a tolerant B-rep is a PL embedding of its cell complex whose cells stay in their tolerance
zones.

\emph{Uniqueness.} Suppose every shell has genus at least one, the carriers of the cells are contractible, and each
shell has a product collar containing its zones and a reference surface whose inclusion is a homotopy equivalence,
with the collars pairwise disjoint. Then
the images of all realizations are ambient isotopic, and so are the solids they bound. The collar condition follows
from a distance bound below the weak feature size of a reference surface; one step relies on Moise's theorem that
homeomorphic 3-manifolds are PL homeomorphic, whose citation is still to be checked against the original. For polyhedral reference surfaces we give an explicit lower bound for the weak feature size in terms of
conical radii; the key lemma is machine-checked in Lean.

\emph{Existence.} Two constructions give a realization under stated hypotheses. The first uses fans in the normal
disks of the edges and cones at the vertices; it is a conditional theorem whose hypotheses include two non-local
ones. The second, for sharp edges with tight tolerances, re-intersects the face
surfaces.

\emph{Non-existence.} Under explicit conditions, if two planar patches cross transversally away from the shadows of
all edge and vertex zones, there is no realization.

\emph{Experiments.} OpenCascade's validity checks decide none of these questions. An ordinary sewing operation
produces a wedge that passes both of OpenCascade's checks and has no realization. A thick pipe that passes both
checks has two realizations whose solids are not homeomorphic.
\end{abstract}
